Prompt: the look below + ", dark fantasy creature, side view facing right in profile, full body, muted desaturated palette, detailed clean shading"; side view, single black outline, highly detailed, no background.

Avoided: ordinary animals and cute elementals (the least-liked: Moss Wolf, Flicker Wisp, Amber Wasp, Flame Imp, Bellow Toad, Dire Wolf).
