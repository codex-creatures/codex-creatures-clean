# tunnel_wyrm. Front 107x108. Bands: 68,73,83,81,78,68,99,106,106,94
Body plan: giant stone-plated worm rising in an S from a coiled base: dark grey stone plates with
glowing orange rune marks, spiky stone ridges along the back, a big round toothed mouth on the front
of the head (facing right), the tail tip at the lower right.
- Back: the rising body from behind, the runed plates and the spiky back ridges prominent; the back
  of the head, its spiky ridges, pointing away to the upper right; the coiled base; the tail tip.
- MUST NOT appear: the round mouth, the teeth.
