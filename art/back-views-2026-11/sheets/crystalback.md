# crystalback. Front 85x72. Bands: 41,51,59,65,70,77,83,85,71,51
Body plan: stocky armoured quadruped (ankylosaur-like): grey-green plated shell with pale crystal
spikes (lavender-blue) rising from it, head at the front, short tail at the back, stubby legs.
- Near (right): right hind and fore legs. Far legs hidden.
- Back: the rear end and the short tail nearest; the crystal-studded shell seen from behind is the
  main show; the head hidden behind the bulk, at most the snout tip on the right.
- MUST NOT appear: the eye, the face.
