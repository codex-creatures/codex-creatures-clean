# rust_beetle. Front 81x68. Bands: 42,58,59,65,68,71,73,81,80,65
Body plan: domed beetle: rust-brown shell with blue-grey metal rims and red rivet studs, a grey
armoured head-plate at the front with orange horn prongs on top, six thin dark legs splayed.
- Near: the near three legs, larger; far legs splay out on the left under the shell, smaller.
- Back: the rear of the domed shell, its rim and rivets, nearest and largest; the orange horn
  prongs peek over the top of the dome on the right.
- MUST NOT appear: the face/head plate front, red eye-studs on the head, mandibles.
