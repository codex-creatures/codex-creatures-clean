# bellow_toad. Front 90x68. Bands: 18,41,61,74,81,84,88,86,84,67
Body plan: squat sitting toad: warty green back with brown blotches, pale belly, a huge green glassy
throat bubble under its chin dripping slime. Eye domes on top of the head.
- Near (right): the big folded right hind leg and right foreleg.
- Far (left): the left legs hidden under the body.
- Back: the broad warty back with its blotches is the main show; the eye domes seen from behind as
  bumps on top (brown lids, no iris); the throat bubble bulges out past the head at the right, partly
  visible (it's wider than the head), slime drips at its lower edge.
- MUST NOT appear: the eye's iris/pupil, the mouth, the pale belly.
