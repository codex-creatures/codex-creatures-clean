from the hood's back (upper left of src), long thin arms with claws reaching forward and down (to
the right), the body fading into a long wispy tail that sweeps down and curls at the lower right.
- Near: the near arm reaching forward-down, its claws at the right.
- Far: the far arm mostly hidden; its claws may peek.
- Back: the back of the hood, a big rounded cowl with the ragged strips trailing back towards us; the
  ghostly robe's back; the wispy tail sweeping under.
- MUST NOT appear: the skull face, eyes, the hood's opening.
